REAP Books Setup Privacy Terms Source

Privacy

REAP Books runs on your computer. There is no REAP server, no account, and no service behind it. We do not receive your data, because there is nowhere for it to be received.

What we collect

Nothing. REAP has no telemetry, no analytics, no error reporting and no licence check. It does not contact any server operated by us, because we do not operate one.

What REAP stores, and where

All of it is on your own computer, in a folder you chose:

  • Your business profile — your name and address, and, if you enter them, your tax ID and bank details. Stored in profile.json, readable only by your user account.
  • Your receipts and bank statements — the files you put in the workspace folder. REAP reads them; it does not move or upload them.
  • The invoices REAP generates — written into the workspace as PDF and HTML.
  • A Google sign-in token, if you connect a ledger. Stored in your operating system's configuration directory, readable only by your user account, deliberately outside the workspace folder so it cannot be committed to version control by accident.

Google, if you connect one

Connecting a ledger is optional. If you do, REAP signs in to your Google account from your machine — the sign-in happens between you and Google, and the resulting token is stored locally as described above.

REAP asks for a single permission: drive.file, which grants access only to files the application itself created. It creates one spreadsheet — your ledger — and writes your invoices and expenses into it. It cannot see, open or alter any other file in your Google Drive, including your other spreadsheets.

That spreadsheet is yours, in your Drive, under your account. Google's handling of it is governed by Google's privacy policy.

You can withdraw REAP's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file signs REAP out on that machine.

The AI assistant you use REAP with

REAP is a tool that an AI assistant of your choosing operates on your behalf. That assistant is separate software, run by whoever makes it, under its own privacy policy. What passes between you and it is between you and them.

What REAP does control is what it hands the assistant:

  • Never your tax ID, and never your bank details. The assistant receives a redacted view of your profile in which those two are replaced by a flag saying they exist. The real values go from your machine onto the invoice document without passing through the assistant. This holds for the setup interview too: REAP refuses to accept a tax ID or a routing number that way, and tells you to enter it in your terminal instead.
  • Ordinary invoice and expense information does pass through it — client names, line item descriptions, amounts, dates — because that is what you are asking it to help you with. If your assistant retains conversations, that information is in them. Choose your assistant with that in mind.

What we share

Nothing, with anybody. We hold nothing to share. REAP is not funded by advertising, does not sell data, and has no third-party services embedded in it.

Children

REAP is a business bookkeeping tool and is not directed at children.

Checking any of this

You will not have to take our word for it. REAP is open source under Apache-2.0 at github.com/talktalkmake/reapbooks — private while it is pre-release, public at launch — and the claims above are enforced by tests that run on every change, including one that fails the build if any address other than Google's own API endpoints appears in the source code.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published here with a new date. Since REAP holds no data, there is nothing we could retroactively begin doing with data already collected.
